About

I'm teaching myself AI safety, one concept at a time. I'm especially drawn to sycophancy in low-resource languages like Mongolian, where models are less tested and their failures are easier to miss. More broadly, I'm interested in interpretability, alignment and evaluations, and in how safety research can shape AI governance.

Beyond that, I care a lot about the climate and the planet we share.

I'm from Mongolia, and I've lived in Mumbai, India, and spent several years in Tokyo, Japan. Along the way I've traveled to 17 countries and lived in 6. These days, I'm also learning to enjoy a little more stillness.

For now, I'm based in Ulaanbaatar and open to remote opportunities across the APAC region.

Change is the only constant.
